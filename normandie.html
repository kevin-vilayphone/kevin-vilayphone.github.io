Normandy - 2026

Mont Saint-Michel and countryside

In this series, I explore the Normandy region, where the world-renowned Mont Saint-Michel is located. This famous UNESCO World Heritage Site captivates through its abbey, the hypnotizing colours of the surrounding sands, and the flocks of sheep that wander all around the bay. However, Normandy is not limited to this monument, as the region also offers great views typical of France's countryside.
