Alsace - 2024

Strasbourg and local villages

Alsace, located in the north-east of France, is one of the most charming regions of the country. Well-known for its culinary specialties such as choucroute, flammekueche or gingerbread, this region is also renowned for its colourful and centuries-old half-timbered buildings. All of Alsace's specialties are gathered in the region's capital, Strasbourg, at the center of which stands one of the most beautiful cathedrals in the world, easily recognizable by the complex and beautifully sculpted ornaments of its facade, made of pinkish sandstone.
