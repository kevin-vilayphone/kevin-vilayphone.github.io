Occitania - 2026

Rocamadour, Gouffre de Padirac, Cordes-sur-Ciel and Albi

Occitania, in the south-west of France, is a region known for its gastronomy and wine, but also for its rich cultural heritage and architecture spanning centuries. From the troglodyte village of Rocamadour, the vertiginous Padirac Chasm, and the charming village of Cordes-sur-Ciel, to the city of Albi, this series presents some of the views that Occitania has to offer.
